# Code of conduct

Questlight is a small fan project made for fun. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues,
pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is asked to follow this.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are players, not programmers, and are writing in a
  second language or using GitHub for the first time.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with an idea by explaining what you would
  do instead.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully when you get something wrong.
- Keep it about the project. Questions about the game are welcome; unrelated promotion is
  not.
- Mind spoilers. Put story spoilers behind a warning.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or attacks on anyone for who they are, including their age, body,
  disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion, sexual
  orientation, or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome personal attention.
- Sharing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing a conversation, or repeatedly ignoring a request to
  stop.
- Posting copyrighted game artwork or copied text.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this, you can:

- use **Report content** in the "…" menu on any comment, issue or discussion, which sends
  it privately to GitHub, or
- contact the maintainer, [@OldWyrFrm](https://github.com/OldWyrFrm), through GitHub.

Reports are read by the maintainer and handled as privately as possible.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a conversation, or, for serious or
repeated problems, block someone from the project. The aim is always to keep the space
friendly, not to punish.

## Scope

This applies to all of the project's spaces on GitHub, and to anyone acting on the
project's behalf elsewhere.
